Patient Information

Everything you need to know about visiting Greater Hartford Pediatrics.

Scheduling

Call 860-865-3521 to schedule. Our office is open Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm. Dr. Thomas is available for appointments Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays, 8:15am to 4:30pm.

Insurance

We accept HUSKY/Medicaid and most major insurance plans. Please bring your child’s insurance card to every visit.

Ages we see

Newborns, children, teens, and young adults through age 21.

Take a Tour

From the play table in our waiting room to our care team’s station, our office was set up to help children feel at ease.

Our Services

Well-child visits

Check-ups with growth monitoring, developmental screening, and preventive guidance.

Sick visits

Same-day and next-day appointments for illness, infections, and minor injuries.

Immunizations

The full recommended vaccine schedule and seasonal flu shots.

Newborn care

Early newborn visits and breastfeeding support for new families.

Developmental screening

Early identification of developmental delays, autism concerns, and learning differences.

Asthma care

Asthma action plans, inhaler teaching, and coordination with school nurses.

Healthy weight & nutrition

Family-based support for healthy growth, nutrition, and activity.

Behavioral & mental health

Screening and care for ADHD, anxiety, and depression.

Adolescent medicine

Confidential care for teens and young adults, and support for the move to adult care.

Sports & school physicals

School, camp, and sports clearance physicals and forms.

Telehealth

Video visits for follow-ups, medication checks, and non-urgent questions.

Specialist coordination

Referrals to and follow-up with Connecticut Children’s specialists.

Message our office, request appointments, and view your child’s health information securely online.
