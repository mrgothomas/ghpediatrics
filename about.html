Board-certified Pediatrician

Latesha Thomas, MD

Founder & Pediatrician

Dr. Latesha Thomas is a native of Connecticut. She attended the University of Connecticut for her undergraduate and medical education and Connecticut Children’s for residency. U-C-O-N-N! UCONN! UCONN! UCONN! Go Blue!

Dr. Thomas has practiced primary care for more than 18 years, at UConn primary care and then Connecticut Children’s primary care in East Hartford. During that time, she has provided pediatric clinical education for medical students and resident doctors.

Dr. Thomas feels the role of the pediatrician is to educate and empower parents to support the health and development of their children. She works with her adolescent patients to prepare them to participate in their healthcare and to prepare for their future. Dr. Thomas also enjoys supporting new parents and providing breastfeeding counseling.

Dr. Thomas and her husband are the proud parents of three sons with lots of boy energy. She enjoys spending time with family and friends, and also enjoys basketball, baking, learning new things, travel, and shopping. Another important part of Dr. Thomas’s life is her Christian faith, from which she draws her strength.

Dr. Thomas chose to start her private practice to better meet the individual needs of her patients. She also wanted to create an environment that feels like family.

I earned my medical assistant diploma from Porter and Chester Institute and have worked in family medicine and at a pediatric clinic for children with developmental delays. As an aunt to six nieces and one nephew, I love children, and pediatrics is where my heart is.

Our Mission

We provide exceptional, individualized, family-centered care that is culturally sensitive. This philosophy is shared by everyone on our team, from the physician to the front desk.

We enjoy caring for our patients, from newborns to young adults, and building lasting connections with their families. We support and educate parents, and we guide young adults to become advocates for their own health.

We believe in preventing disease and promoting healthy lifestyles for all children, and we work to address the social factors that affect a child’s health.

Individualized care

Care that addresses the unique needs of each child and family.

Collaboration

Working with other medical experts and with families so every patient has the best possible outcome.

Integrity

Honesty, confidentiality, trust, respect, and transparency in every interaction.

Greater Hartford Pediatrics

An independent pediatric practice caring for newborns, children, teens, and young adults in Wethersfield and across Greater Hartford.
