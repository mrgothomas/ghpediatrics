About us

Caring for Children, Partnering with Families

Greater Hartford Pediatrics is an independent, physician-led practice where every family is known by name and every child receives individualized care.

Welcome to Our Practice Family

Our mission

To deliver compassionate, culturally competent, and comprehensive pediatric care to every child in the Greater Hartford community, meeting families where they are and giving every child the healthiest possible start.

We provide exceptional, individualized, family-centered care that is culturally sensitive. This philosophy is shared by everyone on our team, from the physician to the front desk.

We enjoy caring for our patients, from newborns to young adults, and building lasting connections with their families. We support and educate parents, and we guide young adults to become advocates for their own health, always with compassion.

We deliver high-quality, comprehensive care through life-long learning and the best available technology. We believe in preventing disease and promoting healthy lifestyles for all children, and we work to address the social factors that affect a child’s health while supporting healthy physical and social-emotional development.

Our Vision

  • ✓Individualized care that addresses the unique needs of each family.
  • ✓Collaboration with other medical experts and with families, so every patient receives the care that leads to the best possible outcome.
  • ✓Working alongside our community to improve the lives of our patients and their families.
Our commitment to integrity

We hold ourselves to high moral and professional standards. Honesty, confidentiality, trust, respect, and transparency guide every interaction with your family.
