About Our Practice

A Practice Built Around Your Child's Best Health

Greater Hartford Pediatrics is a new, independent pediatric practice committed to delivering outstanding, whole-child care in a welcoming, stress-free environment. We believe every child deserves personalized attention and a physician who truly knows them.

Our state-of-the-art facility is being thoughtfully designed to make even anxious little ones feel at ease — from our child-friendly waiting areas to our warm, unhurried appointments.

  • ✓ Board-certified pediatrician with extensive experience in child and adolescent health
  • ✓ Accepting patients from newborns through young adults (0–21 years)
  • ✓ Welcoming and inclusive environment for all families
  • ✓ Same-day and next-day sick visit appointments
  • ✓ Telehealth services available
  • ✓ Most major insurance plans accepted, including HUSKY/Medicaid
What We Provide

Comprehensive Pediatric Services

Everything your child needs, from their very first well-visit through their teenage years — all in one caring, convenient practice.

📋

Well-Child Visits

Regular developmental screenings, growth monitoring, vaccines, and preventive guidance at every stage of childhood.

🤒

Sick Visits

Prompt same-day and next-day appointments for illness, infections, injuries, and urgent concerns.

💉

Immunizations

Full CDC-recommended vaccine schedule, travel vaccines, and flu shots to keep your child protected.

🧠

Developmental Screenings

Early identification of developmental delays, autism spectrum concerns, ADHD, learning differences, and behavioral needs.

💻

Telehealth Visits

Convenient video appointments for follow-ups, non-urgent questions, and families who can't make it into the office.

🏃

Sports Physicals

School and sports clearance physicals completed quickly to keep your young athlete on the field.

🌙

Newborn Care

Post-birth care, breastfeeding support, newborn screenings, and early developmental guidance for new families.

🤝

Adolescent Medicine

Confidential, respectful care for teens covering mental health, nutrition, sexual health, and the transition to adulthood.
